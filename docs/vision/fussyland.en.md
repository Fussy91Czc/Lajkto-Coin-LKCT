# LKCT and FussyLand: A Long-Term Vision

**FussyCraft | Founder of Lajkto Coin (LKCT) and initiator of FussyLand**

Lajkto Coin (LKCT) is an independent proof-of-work blockchain project. Alongside its technical development, I am developing a long-term concept for FussyLand, a proposed new town in South Bohemia, Czech Republic.

The current pre-investment concept envisages phased housing and services for approximately 25,000 residents, employment, industry, education, healthcare and infrastructure. This is a proposal, not an approved or funded construction project.

## A potential role for my LKCT holdings

I am considering using part of **my personally held and mined LKCT** as one possible future funding source for the preparation and phased development of FussyLand. This would depend on actual market conditions and the ability to sell those coins for realizable proceeds.

This does not involve other holders' assets or automatically direct blockchain network funds to the town. LKCT and FussyLand are separate projects. Holding LKCT provides no ownership interest in FussyLand, claim to its revenues or governance rights over its development.

## A realistic, phased approach

The scope and pace of any development would depend on financing, land availability, planning, permits and economic feasibility. Individual elements may be phased, revised or postponed.
