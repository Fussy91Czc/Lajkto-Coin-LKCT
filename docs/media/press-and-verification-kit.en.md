## Suggested independent verification

1. Examine the actual consensus source and monetary-policy audit script in this repository; compare parameters with this page.
2. Follow the published node instructions to connect to the LKCT mainnet and independently query block height, chain identity and peer connectivity.
3. Inspect the public pool statistics endpoint, noting that pool figures are not equivalent to entire-network statistics.
4. Verify wallet binaries and any published checksums or signatures before installation. If no independently verifiable release signatures are available, report that limitation.
5. Check supply, genesis allocation, issuance and current chain statistics against a fully synchronized node rather than extrapolating from a short mining interval.
6. Treat any claims about adoption, exchange listing, price, liquidity, audited security or third-party wallet support as unverified unless independently documented.

## FussyLand: separate conceptual project

FussyLand is a **conceptual pre-investment plan**, not a financed or permitted city development. The current planning concept describes an initial city for approximately 25,000 residents and 10,000 rental apartments in South Bohemia, Czech Republic. Founder FussyCraft is considering whether part of their personally held or mined LKCT might become one possible future funding source, **only if real liquidity, legal feasibility and economic conditions permit**.

Owning LKCT does not confer ownership of, equity in, or claims on FussyLand. There is no guaranteed LKCT price, return, exchange listing or financing commitment.
